# Capability Blueprint: Proactive Ambient Automation

- **Inspired by**: `dev-core-busy/jarvis` (Short Tracks Runner), `kar-ganap/jarvis` (Scheduler), `Friday` (Care Reminders)

---

## 1. Urgency-Ranked Ambient Check-ins
Instead of waiting passively for user prompts, the proactive companion evaluates commitments periodically and surfaces actionable notifications:

* `[Urgency 9/10]` Capstone Architecture Documentation due tomorrow at 5:00 PM.
* `[Urgency 8/10]` Rahul Sharma awaiting reply regarding Capstone project files.
* `[Urgency 7/10]` Capstone Demonstration scheduled for 10:30 AM in Lab 402.

---

## 2. Notification Delivery Channels
* Rendered on the Waybar topbar.
* Displayed on the Holographic HUD right-side companion card.
* Optional subtle audio chime via PipeWire audio sink.
