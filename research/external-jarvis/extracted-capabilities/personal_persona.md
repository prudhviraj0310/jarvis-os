# Capability Blueprint: Personal Persona & Provenance Engine

- **Inspired by**: `Friday` (Circle Model), `yashvar23/jarvis` (User Model), `MIRA` (Memory Wiki)

---

## 1. Provenance Tagging & Evidence Separation
In JARVIS OS, information stored in the personal memory graph is explicitly divided by certainty:
* `[OBSERVED]`: Concrete verifiable facts witnessed by the system (e.g. `[OBSERVED, 2026-08-21 07:45] Rahul requested project files via WhatsApp`).
* `[DERIVED]`: Synthesized mathematical calculations (e.g. `[DERIVED] Attendance at 87.5% gives +3 classes safety buffer`).
* `[INFERRED]`: Hypothesized preferences or communication tones (e.g. `[INFERRED, confidence=0.88] Rahul prefers casual short replies; Prof. Krishnamurthy prefers formal titles`).

---

## 2. Dynamic Tone Modulation
* When drafting replies:
  * **Peers / Friends**: Brief, casual, informal phrasing.
  * **Faculty / Administration**: Formal, structured, respectful academic tone.
