# Capability Blueprint: Daily Executive Intelligence Briefing

- **Inspired by**: `Raghava001/Jarvis` (Morning HUD Greeting), `Friday` (Daily Digest), `kar-ganap/jarvis` (Scheduler Engine)

---

## 1. Structure of the Deep Morning Briefing
When the user boots the OS or logs in each morning, the `ContextEngine` assembles a comprehensive intelligence report:

1. **Audio Greeting & Time**: Adaptive based on local time and user name (`"Good morning, Prudhvi Raj"`).
2. **Schedule & Countdown**: Today's meetings, reviews, and classes with locations.
3. **Communication Highlights**: Actionable WhatsApp messages and high-priority emails requiring response.
4. **Academic & Performance Telemetry**: Real-time attendance percentage, exam hall ticket status, and safe-zone class buffer.
5. **Curated World News**: Top headlines across AI, Technology, and India.
6. **Proposed Actions**: Direct interactive chips (`'Handle It'`, `'Launch Web Browser'`).
